Required disclosures

Scope of service

Balanced Life Care is a care coordination service collaborating with licensed independent physicians and advanced practice clinicians. Balanced Life Care does not directly provide medical services, does not practice medicine or pharmacy, and does not guarantee any clinical outcome.

Consultation and prescription

A consultation with a licensed clinician is required before any prescription medication is prescribed or dispensed. Payment does not ensure prescription issuance. Prescriptions are issued at the sole discretion of the treating clinician based on medical necessity.

Licensure

All treating clinicians hold active, unrestricted licenses in the state in which the patient is located at the time of the visit. License verification is available on request at support@balancedlife-care.com. Pharmacy partners are licensed in the states they serve; pharmacy name and license number appear on your dispensing label.

Controlled substances

Controlled substances are prescribed only in accordance with the Ryan Haight Online Pharmacy Consumer Protection Act, DEA regulations, and applicable state law. We do not prescribe opioids, stimulants, or benzodiazepines through this service. Testosterone (Schedule III) requires confirmatory laboratory testing and ongoing monitoring.

Eligibility

Services are available only to individuals 18 years of age or older who are physically located in the United States at the time of the visit. Certain treatments are unavailable in certain states.

Adverse events and complaints

Report side effects to your clinician immediately and to the FDA MedWatch program at 1-800-FDA-1088 or fda.gov/medwatch. Complaints about this service may be directed to support@balancedlife-care.com and are acknowledged within two business days.
